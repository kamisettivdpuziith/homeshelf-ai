# HomeShelf AI

### Know what you have. Use what you need. Buy only what you need.

HomeShelf AI is an AI-powered household inventory assistant designed to help users track household products, identify items that should be used soon, monitor low-stock items, and make smarter shopping decisions.

## Problem

People may forget what household products they already have, overlook items approaching their user-entered expiry dates, and purchase products they already own. This can lead to unnecessary spending and avoidable household waste.

## Solution

HomeShelf AI maintains a digital household inventory and combines rule-based inventory analysis with AI-powered recommendations.

Users can add household items with information such as:

- Item name
- Category
- Quantity
- Purchase date
- Expiry date
- Minimum stock level

The system then organizes the inventory and provides useful recommendations.

## Core Features

- 🏠 Household inventory
- 🔴 Expiry and priority alerts
- 🟡 Low-stock detection
- 🤖 AI recommendations
- 🍳 Meal suggestions
- 🛒 Smart shopping list
